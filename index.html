RewardStar XL Android

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RewardStar XL (developed by Empire Pixels Pro) handles information when you use our Android application. RewardStar XL has no backend server — all of your data stays safely on your device. By using the app, you agree to the use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Target Audience & Age Appropriateness

RewardStar XL is intended for an adult audience aged 18 and over — parents, guardians, teachers and tutors. It is a management tool that lets an adult record a child's positive behaviour as stars, set task targets, and award rewards such as screen time or a treat once the agreed number of stars has been reached.

Children may naturally be interested in the star and reward concept they can see, but the app is installed and operated on the adult's own phone, not on a child's device. To stop a child from awarding stars to themselves, the adult-facing areas of the app are protected by a parent PIN and fingerprint / device biometric lock.

We do not knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from anyone under 18. The app contains no account registration, no child profile that stores personal identifiers, and no mechanism for a child to submit information to us.

2. Who Is in Control

One adult account holder installs the app and controls it. Child profiles created inside the app are simple nicknames used for scoring on that same device. The adult can edit or delete them at any time from within the app.

3. Information the App Accesses

The app accesses the following only on your device, and only with your permission where required:

  • Local storage: child profiles, tasks, star / point ledgers, rewards, goals, history and settings are written to the app's own on-device database. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Camera & photos (optional): only if you choose to pick or take a picture for a profile avatar. The image is resized and stored locally.
  • Fingerprint / biometric (optional): used solely to unlock the parent-protected areas of the app. Biometric data never leaves the device and is never readable by us.
  • Internet: used only to load advertisements and to process Google Play in-app purchases.
  • Home-screen widgets: read your locally stored star totals and task lists to display them on the launcher.
  • What we do NOT collect: we do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, contacts, precise location, call logs, or unique device identifiers to any server we operate — the app has no backend of its own.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To record good-job and misbehaviour events and calculate star / point balances.
  • To generate the progress reports, charts and leaderboards you see in the app.
  • To remind you when a reward target is reached or a task is due.
  • To power your home-screen widgets.
  • To process your optional PRO upgrade securely through Google Play Billing — payment details are never seen or stored by us.

5. Advertising

RewardStar XL may display Google AdMob advertisements. Google may collect advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. No other data is shared with advertisers. Ads are shown only in the free version; upgrading to PRO removes them.

6. In-App Purchases

Optional PRO upgrades are sold as one-time in-app products and are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive, process or store your card or payment details, and we do not operate any payment system of our own.

7. Data Security & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. Parent controls are protected by a salted PIN hash with brute-force lockout, plus optional fingerprint unlock. Any network traffic the app makes is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Data Deletion: you retain full control. Erase everything with the in-app reset options, delete individual child profiles from the profiles screen, or simply uninstall the app — all records are removed with it. To request removal of any technical support records, contact us by email with your identifier.
